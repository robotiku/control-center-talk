Teams and each other

Teams do not wait for each other. They agree, then work in parallel.

Guarantees team

Guarantees plugin

Knows the guarantee status of every order.

The platform passes the information from one plugin to the other.
Step 1: agree on the question. "For this order, is there buyer protection: yes or no?" Both teams sign off on that one sentence.
Step 2: work in parallel. Reviews builds the display with test data. Guarantees builds the real answer. Nobody waits.
Step 3: connect. When Guarantees is ready, the platform connects the two. Each team keeps its ownership.
Where I come inI help two teams find that one sentence and make sure the connection goes through the platform, not around it. That is what keeps 8 teams from stepping on each other.
This is why one team's release day is not another team's problem.7 / 8
